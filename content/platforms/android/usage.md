## Performance tweaks

- Tweak the background usage limits option: Head over to Settings -> Device care -> Battery -> Background usage limits. This screen controls most of the app-killing behavior on your phone. Here are the options you will see

    - Put unused apps to sleep: Turn this off to stop the automatic sweeping entirely.
    - Never sleeping apps: You can add the most-used apps to this list and permanently exempt them from sleeping.
    - Sleeping apps: Apps that are currently napping and will wake up if you open them.
    - Deep sleeping apps: Apps that are fully restricted and won’t do anything in the background. 
    
    (Source: https://www.makeuseof.com/turned-off-samsungs-aggressive-app-killer-galaxy-finally-felt-responsive/)

- Turn off Adaptive Battery optimization and Auto optimization: While in the Background usage limits option, tap the three-dot icon in the top-right corner and select Adaptive battery. If you think your phone is delivering good battery life, as most high-end smartphones do, you can turn off the Adaptive battery option. This way, the apps will behave predictably instead of intelligently using AI. Inside Device Care, you will also see the Auto optimization option. This feature periodically restarts your device and force-closes background apps on a schedule to keep things running smoothly. However, instead of keeping things on the smoother side, it interrupts anything you had running and resets apps you wanted to stay open. When you turn the Auto optimization feature off, your phone won’t be quietly rebooting itself or closing apps while you aren’t looking. (Source: https://www.makeuseof.com/turned-off-samsungs-aggressive-app-killer-galaxy-finally-felt-responsive/)

- Say goodbye to RAM Plus: Plus. Samsung has a feature, RAM Plus, enabled by default, that acts as virtual memory. To do that, head to Settings -> Device Care -> Memory -> RAM Plus. Here, when you turn off RAM Plus, your device will prompt you to restart. Proceed, and RAM Plus will be turned off. (Source: https://www.makeuseof.com/turned-off-samsungs-aggressive-app-killer-galaxy-finally-felt-responsive/)
