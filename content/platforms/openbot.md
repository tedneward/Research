We have designed a small electric vehicle that costs about $50 and serves as a robot body. Our software stack for Android smartphones supports advanced robotics workloads such as person following and real-time autonomous navigation.

## Getting started

-   Read the [Disclaimer](https://github.com/ob-f/OpenBot/blob/master/DISCLAIMER.md)
-   Build your own [Robot Body](https://github.com/ob-f/OpenBot/blob/master/body/README.md)
-   Flash the [Arduino Firmware](https://github.com/ob-f/OpenBot/blob/master/firmware/README.md)
-   Install the [Robot App](https://github.com/ob-f/OpenBot/blob/master/robot/README.md)
-   Drive the robot via a [Controller](https://github.com/ob-f/OpenBot/blob/master/controller/README.md)
-   Program your robot in the [Playground](https://github.com/ob-f/OpenBot/blob/master/open-code/README.md)
-   Train your own [Driving Policy](https://github.com/ob-f/OpenBot/blob/master/policy/README.md)
